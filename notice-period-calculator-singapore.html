Short answer

In Singapore a notice period starts on the day notice is given and includes rest days and public holidays. One month's notice given on 15 July 2025 ends on 14 August 2025; given on 30 January 2025 it ends on 28 February 2025, because February is shorter (MOM). If the contract says nothing, the Employment Act minimum is 1 day for less than 26 weeks of service, 1 week up to 2 years, 2 weeks up to 5 years and 4 weeks after that.

How to count a notice period in Singapore

  • Days: the last day is the notice date plus the number of days, minus one, because the first day counts.
  • Weeks: the notice date plus 7 days per week, minus one. Two weeks given on Monday 2 March 2026 end on Sunday 15 March 2026.
  • Months: the day before the same date in the final month. One month from 15 July ends on 14 August. If the final month has no such date, it ends on that month's last day: one month from 30 January 2025 ends on 28 February 2025.

Rest days and public holidays inside the period still count (MOM, termination with notice). Your contract, or your company's policy, may require longer notice than the minimum, and the contract always comes first.

Salary in lieu of notice

Either side can end the contract without serving the notice by paying salary in lieu: the salary that would have been earned in the notice period. For part of a period, MOM's daily gross rate of pay = 12 x monthly gross rate of pay / (52 x working days a week) (MOM, monthly and daily salary). Gross rate of pay includes contractual allowances but excludes overtime, bonuses, AWS, productivity incentives, special expense reimbursements and travel, food and housing allowances.

Worked example: a monthly gross salary of S$3,900 on a 5-day week gives a daily gross rate of 12 x 3,900 / (52 x 5) = S$180. Leaving with 5 working days of notice unserved means S$900 in lieu. CPF contributions are not required on salary in lieu of notice.

Using annual leave to offset notice

With the employer's agreement, unused annual leave can be used to bring the last working day forward. The employee is paid up to the last day of work, and the leave used to offset is not paid out separately. Enter the leave days in the calculator to see the earlier last working day; public holidays in between push it later, so check the calendar for the year.

Questions

Does the notice period include weekends and public holidays? Yes. MOM counts the notice period in calendar days, including rest days, non-working days and public holidays.

Is the day I resign counted? Yes. The day notice is given is the first day of the notice period, so one month of notice given on 15 July ends on 14 August.

Can I take leave during my notice period? With your employer's agreement you can use annual leave to offset the notice period and leave earlier. You are paid up to your last day of work, and the leave used this way is not paid out.

How is salary in lieu of notice calculated? It is the salary you would have earned in the notice period you did not serve. For part of a period, MOM's daily gross rate of pay is 12 x monthly gross rate of pay divided by (52 x working days a week). CPF contributions are not required on salary in lieu of notice.

What if my contract says nothing about notice? The Employment Act minimum applies: 1 day for less than 26 weeks of service, 1 week for 26 weeks to less than 2 years, 2 weeks for 2 to less than 5 years, and 4 weeks for 5 years or more.

Does notice have to be in writing? Yes. MOM says termination notice must be in writing, such as a letter or an email, and a waiver of notice by mutual agreement should also be in writing.

Is unused annual leave paid out when I resign? Yes, unless the employee is dismissed for misconduct. MOM says the employer must pay for unused annual leave at the gross rate of pay, based on the last drawn salary. Leave used to offset the notice period is not paid again.
