Annual Leave Calculator (Singapore)

Work out annual leave the way MOM does: 7 days in the first year rising to 14, pro-rated in the first 12 months, the balance after carry-forward and leave taken, and what unused leave is worth when someone leaves. Free, no signup, nothing leaves your browser.

Short answer

Under the Employment Act, annual leave is 7 days in the first year of service, rising by one day a year to 14 days from the 8th year. It starts after 3 months of service. In the first 12 months it is pro-rated: completed months ÷ 12 × 7, with under half a day rounded down and half or more rounded up, so 8 completed months gives 5 days (MOM).

When employment ends for any reason other than misconduct, unused leave must be paid at the gross rate of pay: 12 × monthly gross salary ÷ (52 × days worked a week), which is S$152.31 a day on S$3,300 gross for a 5-day week.

Annual leave entitlement in Singapore

Employees covered by the Employment Act who have worked for at least 3 months get paid annual leave by year of service:

Days7891011121314

A year of service runs from the start date, not the calendar year: someone who joined on 15 January 2021 starts their 6th year on 15 January 2026. A contract can give more than these minimums, never less.

Pro-rated annual leave in the first year

Between 3 and 12 months of service, including probation, the leave is pro-rated: (completed months of service ÷ 12) × the year's entitlement. A fraction under half a day is dropped; half a day or more counts as a full day.

Worked examples

1. First year. Joined 1 March 2026, worked out on 1 November 2026: 8 completed months. 8 ÷ 12 × 7 = 4.67 days, which rounds up to 5 days.

2. Sixth year, with carry-forward. Joined 15 January 2021, worked out on 20 November 2026: 70 completed months, so the 6th year of service and 12 days. With 2 days carried forward and 6 taken, the balance is 12 + 2 − 6 = 8 days.

3. Paying out unused leave. The same employee resigns with those 8 days unused, on a S$3,300 monthly gross salary and a 5-day week. Gross rate per day = 12 × 3,300 ÷ (52 × 5) = 39,600 ÷ 260 = S$152.31. Leave pay = 8 × 152.31 = S$1,218.46.

Carry forward, forfeiture and leaving

  • Carry forward: for staff under Part 4 of the Employment Act, the employer must allow unused leave to be carried forward to the next 12 months; if it is not used by then it can be forfeited (MOM, updated 24 July 2025). For everyone else the contract decides.
  • Absence: statutory leave can be forfeited if the employee is absent without permission or reasonable excuse for more than 20% of the working days in the month or year.
  • Leaving: on resignation or termination for any reason other than misconduct, unused leave is paid at the gross rate of pay, based on the last drawn salary. On dismissal for misconduct it is forfeited (MOM, updated 22 April 2026).
  • Offsetting notice: with the employer's agreement, leave can be used to cover part of the notice period. Leave used that way is not paid out again. The notice period calculator works out the new last day.

Sources: MOM, annual leave eligibility and entitlement (updated 17 April 2023) and MOM, monthly and daily salary (updated 8 December 2025). This calculator does the arithmetic; it is not legal advice.

Questions

How many days of annual leave do employees get in Singapore? Under the Employment Act: 7 days in the first year of service, one more day each year after that, up to 14 days from the 8th year. It applies after 3 months of service.

How is annual leave pro-rated in the first year? Completed months of service divided by 12, times the entitlement. Under half a day is rounded down and half or more is rounded up, so 8 completed months gives 5 days.

Can I take annual leave during probation? Yes, once you have worked 3 months. The pro-rating applies during probation too.

Is unused annual leave paid when I resign? Yes. When employment ends for any reason other than misconduct, the employer must pay for unused annual leave at the gross rate of pay. On dismissal for misconduct it is forfeited.

How is unused annual leave pay calculated? Unused days times the gross rate of pay per day. For a monthly-rated employee the gross rate is 12 x monthly gross salary divided by (52 x days worked a week): S$152.31 a day on S$3,300 for a 5-day week.

Can unused annual leave be carried forward? For staff under Part 4 of the Employment Act, yes: the employer must allow it to be carried forward to the next 12 months, after which it can be forfeited. For others, the contract decides.

Does the year of service follow the calendar year? No. It runs from the employment start date. Many employers run leave on the calendar year for convenience, but the statutory minimum is counted from the start date.
