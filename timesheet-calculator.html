How to calculate hours worked

Take the finish time, subtract the start time, then subtract unpaid breaks. A shift from 8:30am to 5:45pm is 9 hours 15 minutes; minus a 45-minute lunch, that is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.5 hours. If the finish time is earlier than the start time, the shift ran past midnight: 10pm to 6am is 8 hours.

Overtime rules

Singapore: for employees covered by Part IV of the Employment Act, normal hours are up to 44 a week, and overtime is paid at no less than 1.5 times the basic hourly rate. United States: the federal rule is 1.5 times for hours over 40 in a week, and some states also count daily overtime, which is what the "after hours / day" setting is for. Check the rules that apply to your employees; this calculator does the arithmetic, not the legal advice.
