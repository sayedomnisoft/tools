What WATI is

Most small teams start with one phone and the WhatsApp Business app. It works until two people need to answer the same customer, or the phone goes home with someone. WATI moves the business number onto the official WhatsApp Business API and puts every chat into one inbox in a browser. Each chat can be assigned to a person, tagged and followed up, and the owner can see what was said.

On top of the inbox, WATI's plans list broadcasts and drip campaigns, chatbots, a product catalogue with Shopify abandoned-cart and order messages, and an AI agent. You do not need all of it. For most small teams the inbox is the reason to buy and the rest comes later.

Who needs it, and who does not

Likely worth it

  • Two or more people reply to customers on WhatsApp.
  • Customers chase you because nobody answered, or two people answered differently.
  • You send the same update to many customers and do it by hand.
  • You cannot tell who promised what to a customer last week.

Probably not yet

  • One person replies and keeps up.
  • WhatsApp is a side channel and orders come in elsewhere.
  • Nobody will own the inbox. A tool does not fix that.
  • The monthly price is more than the chats you lose are worth.

What the 7-day trial lets you do

The trial is a real look at the product with the risky parts switched off. You can use the team inbox, create and send campaigns, test chatbots and look at the dashboard, and every message goes to your own WhatsApp number. You cannot message customers, submit a template for approval, save an automation workflow or add your team. WATI's trial article says no payment details are needed to start.

Seven days is short, so go in with a list. The 7-day test plan gives you one job a day and the three decisions to choose from on day 7.

What you need to go live

The software is rarely the hold-up. The number is. Before you connect a business number, WATI's setup articles ask for a number that can receive a one-time code and is not on an active WhatsApp account (or one that qualifies for Coexistence), admin access to your Meta Business Manager, a public website that shows who you are, a display name that follows Meta's rules, a laptop with Chrome, and a paid WATI subscription. The go-live checklist lets you tick them off and tells you what is still open.

Four things that catch small teams out

1. Growth stops at 3 users

The Growth plan includes 3 users and has no option to add more. The day a fourth person needs a login, you move to Pro, which is S$210 more a month on yearly billing. If you can see that fourth person coming within the year, price Pro from the start.

2. Meta's charges are on top

WATI's pricing page states that template messages carry additional charges and that the rate depends on whether the message is marketing, utility or authentication. A team that mostly replies to customers will barely notice. A team that sends promotions to thousands every week will.

3. The number has to move

A number that is on an active WhatsApp account has to come off the app before it can connect, unless you qualify for Coexistence, which keeps the WhatsApp Business app on the same number. Decide which route you are taking before you tell customers anything.

4. Messages you start need an approved template

Under Meta's rules for the WhatsApp Business API, you can reply freely while a customer conversation is open, but a message you start has to use a template that Meta has approved. The trial lets you write templates but not submit them, so allow time for approval after you subscribe.

Questions

What is WATI used for?

WATI is a shared team inbox built on the official WhatsApp Business API. Several people can reply to customers from one business number, with chats assigned and tracked, and the same account can send broadcasts and run chatbots.

How much does WATI cost in Singapore?

On WATI's pricing page on 10 November 2026, Growth was S$119 a month billed yearly (S$159 billed monthly) for 3 users, and Pro was S$329 a month billed yearly (S$429 billed monthly) for 5 users, with extra Pro users at S$35 each a month. Template message charges are added on top.

Is WATI free to try?

Yes. WATI offers a 7-day free trial, and its trial article says no payment details are needed. During the trial, messages go to your own WhatsApp number only.

Is WATI worth it for one person?

Usually not. One person replying can stay on the free WhatsApp Business app with quick replies, labels and an away message. A shared inbox starts to pay when two or more people share the same customer chats.

Can I keep using the WhatsApp Business app with WATI?

Only through the Coexistence route, which keeps the WhatsApp Business app on the same number. WATI lists conditions for it, including a recent app version and a supported country. Otherwise the number has to come off the app first.
