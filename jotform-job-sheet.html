For service and site teams still on carbon-copy job sheets. One form on the technician's phone replaces the paper, the retyping and the lost copies. Here is the build, step by step, and the plan you would actually need.

The job this replaces

Illustration, not a client: an aircon servicing firm with three technicians. Each visit ends with a three-part carbon job sheet: the customer signs, keeps the pink copy, the white copy goes back in the van. On Friday someone at the office types the week's sheets into Excel so the invoices can go out. Some sheets arrive late, some never, and a few cannot be read.

Nothing about that is unusual. The work is done and signed for; it just takes days to reach the person who bills it. The form below does not change the job, only where the paper goes.

Build it: seven steps

  1. Create the form. Start a blank form in Jotform and add the elements in the table, in the same order as the paper. The Signature element sits under the Basic tab of the element list. Mark the job number and the signature as required.
  2. Connect Google Sheets. In the Form Builder click Settings, then Integrations, then Google Sheets. Choose Create a New Spreadsheet, click Next, then Authenticate with the Google account the office uses. Keep all fields ticked and click Save. If you are moving a form that already has entries, tick "Send Existing Submissions to the Sheet".
  3. Send the signed PDF to the office. Settings, then Emails, then edit the notification email. On its Advanced tab switch on Attach PDF and click Save. Every submission now arrives in the office inbox with the filled, signed sheet attached.
  4. Send the customer their copy. Under Settings, Emails, add an autoresponder addressed to the Email field. Jotform's help article on attaching the PDF covers notification emails; check the same Advanced tab in your autoresponder before you promise customers a PDF.
  5. Put it on the phones. Share the form link with each technician and add it to the home screen, or install Jotform's free Mobile Forms app, which Jotform says saves entries offline and syncs them when the phone reconnects.
  6. Test with three made-up jobs. Fill it in from a phone, once with no signal. Check that three rows reached the sheet, three PDFs reached the office and the photos open.
  7. Leave the sheet's headers alone. Jotform's help article warns that renaming column headers or adding filters in Google Sheets can pause the updates. Build reports on a second tab that reads from the first.

Steps follow Jotform's help articles on Google Sheets (updated 3 January 2026) and PDF attachments in email notifications (updated 1 June 2024), read on 17 November 2026. Menu names can move; the help centre has the current screens.

Before you start: three honest limits

1. The free plan keeps 500 submissions

At 88 visits a month (one technician, four a day) the free plan's 500 stored submissions fill in under six months. The rows already sent to your Google Sheet stay in Google, but plan how you will archive old entries in Jotform before you reach the cap.

2. Branding stays on the free plan

Jotform's pricing page says forms and signed documents on Starter carry Jotform branding, removed on paid plans. Customers will see it on the form.

3. Signed documents are a separate count

A plain Signature field is a normal submission. With Jotform Sign Automation switched on for that field, Jotform's support team says every submission also counts against the monthly signed documents limit (10 on Starter, 100 on Bronze). Most job sheets do not need Sign Automation; switch it on only if you need its audit trail.

When a form is not enough

A form gets the record to the office the same day. It does not price the job, deduct the stock or raise the invoice. If the sheet starts to become the system, with formulas pulling hours into invoices and materials into reorders, that is the point where job costing and stock belong in proper software. Prefer to stay on free tools? Google Forms costs nothing and also writes to Google Sheets, but it has no built-in signature field, which is the main reason this page uses Jotform.

Questions

Is Jotform free for a job sheet?

Yes, up to a point. The free Starter plan allows 5 forms and 100 submissions a month, keeps 500 submissions in total, gives 100 MB of upload space and shows Jotform branding on your forms. One technician doing about four visits a day fits. A team of three does not.

Can the customer sign on the phone?

Yes. Add the Signature element from the Basic tab in the Form Builder and the customer signs on the screen. If you also switch on Jotform Sign Automation for that field, Jotform's support team says every submission counts against the monthly signed documents limit, which is 10 on the free plan.

Does it work without mobile data?

Jotform says its free Mobile Forms app collects submissions offline and syncs them to your account when the phone reconnects. Test it once in a basement or plant room before you rely on it.

Will the Google Sheet update if I change a submission?

Jotform's help article says edits made in the entry editor reach the sheet, but inline edits in Jotform Tables do not, and renaming the sheet's column headers can pause the updates. Leave the headers alone.

Does it create the invoice?

No. It gives the office a clean, signed record of each visit in one sheet. Raising the invoice is still a separate step, in your accounting software or in the free invoice generator on this site.
