Overtime Calculator (Singapore)

Work out overtime pay the way MOM does: the hourly basic rate from a monthly, daily or hourly basic, 1.5x for every overtime hour, the S$2,600 and S$4,500 coverage limits, the 72-hour cap and the date it must be paid. Free, no signup, nothing leaves your browser.

Short answer

Overtime pay = hourly basic rate × 1.5 × overtime hours. For a monthly-rated employee in Singapore the hourly basic rate is 12 × monthly basic salary ÷ (52 × 44). On a S$2,400 basic salary that is S$12.59 an hour, so 20 hours of overtime pays S$377.62 (MOM).

The legal overtime rules cover non-workmen earning a basic salary of S$2,600 or less a month and workmen earning S$4,500 or less. Overtime is capped at 72 hours a month and must be paid within 14 days after the end of the salary period.

The overtime formula MOM uses

Overtime is paid at at least 1.5 times the hourly basic rate of pay. How you get the hourly basic rate depends on how the employee is paid:

Paid byHourly basic rate
Month(12 × monthly basic rate of pay) ÷ (52 × 44)
Daydaily pay at the basic rate ÷ working hours per day
Piecetotal weekly pay at the basic rate ÷ total hours worked in the week

Use the basic pay only. Allowances, overtime, bonuses and the AWS are not part of the basic rate, so a S$2,400 basic salary with a S$200 transport allowance is still S$2,400 for this formula.

Who is entitled to overtime pay

EmployeeCovered when monthly basic salary is
Non-workman (office, retail, service roles)S$2,600 or less
Workman (manual work)S$4,500 or less
Managers and executivesNot covered by these rules

For non-workmen MOM caps the overtime rate at the S$2,600 salary level, an hourly basic rate of S$13.60. Employees outside these limits get overtime only if their contract says so, on the terms it sets.

Worked examples

1. Non-workman, monthly-rated. Basic salary S$2,400, 20 overtime hours. Hourly basic rate = 12 × 2,400 ÷ (52 × 44) = 28,800 ÷ 2,288 = S$12.59. Overtime rate = 12.59 × 1.5 = S$18.88. Overtime pay = 18.88 × 20 = S$377.62.

2. Workman, monthly-rated. Basic salary S$3,500, 30 overtime hours. Hourly basic rate = 42,000 ÷ 2,288 = S$18.36. Overtime pay = 18.36 × 1.5 × 30 = S$826.05. Covered, because a workman earning up to S$4,500 is.

3. Daily-rated. S$90 a day for an 8-hour day, 10 overtime hours. Hourly basic rate = 90 ÷ 8 = S$11.25. Overtime pay = 11.25 × 1.5 × 10 = S$168.75.

Limits and deadlines

  • Normal hours: up to 44 hours a week; on a 5-day week up to 9 hours a day, on a longer week up to 8 hours a day. Work beyond the normal hours is overtime.
  • Monthly cap: up to 72 overtime hours a month, unless the employer has an exemption from MOM.
  • When to pay: within 14 days after the last day of the salary period. Enter the period end in the calculator to get the date.
  • Payslip: the itemised payslip must show the overtime hours, the overtime pay and the overtime period if it differs from the salary period. The free payslip generator has those fields.

Questions

How is overtime calculated in Singapore? Overtime pay is the hourly basic rate times 1.5 times the overtime hours. For monthly-rated staff the hourly basic rate is 12 x monthly basic salary divided by (52 x 44).

What is the overtime formula for a monthly salary? Hourly basic rate = (12 x monthly basic rate of pay) / (52 x 44). On S$2,400 that is S$12.59, so each overtime hour pays at least S$18.88.

Who is entitled to overtime pay in Singapore? Under Part 4 of the Employment Act: non-workmen with a monthly basic salary of S$2,600 or less and workmen with S$4,500 or less. Managers and executives are not covered by these rules.

Is there a cap on the overtime rate? Yes, for non-workmen. MOM caps their overtime rate at the S$2,600 salary level, an hourly basic rate of S$13.60.

How many overtime hours are allowed a month? Up to 72 hours a month. Employers who need more must apply to MOM for an exemption.

When must overtime be paid? Within 14 days after the last day of the salary period in which the overtime was worked.

Are allowances included in the overtime rate? No. Overtime is worked out from the basic rate of pay, which leaves out allowances, overtime, bonuses and the AWS.
