About Sayed Rashid and The Gantry

I'm Sayed Rashid. I spend my days inside Singapore SMEs, untangling how work actually gets done: quotations in WhatsApp, stock counts in Excel, invoices typed twice, and month-end closes that drag on for weeks.

The Gantry is where I document what I find: the bottlenecks that quietly eat a team's week, the processes worth automating first, and the playbooks that move a business from "everything lives in someone's head" to a system that runs without heroics.

Walk the port at Tanjong Pagar or Tuas and you will see gantry cranes moving loads no person could lift, all day, without drama. That is what a good system does inside a business. The re-typing, the checking and the chasing should sit on the system, not on your people.

What you will find here

Free tools that show how many hours your team loses to manual work, what a slow month-end costs, and whether the business is ready for a proper system. They run in your browser, they are free, and you do not need to give an email to see a result. Alongside them: plain guides on InvoiceNow, grants, stock and invoicing, and short videos that walk through one fix at a time.
