Sick Leave Calculator (Singapore)

Work out paid sick leave the way MOM does: 5 to 14 outpatient days and 15 to 60 hospitalisation days depending on months of service, how many are left this year, the dates each step starts and what a paid sick day costs. Free, no signup, nothing leaves your browser.

Short answer

After 6 months of service, employees covered by the Employment Act get 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave and 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave a year, and the 60 days include the 14. Paid sick leave starts after 3 months: 5 outpatient and 15 hospitalisation days at 3 months, 8 and 30 at 4 months, 11 and 45 at 5 months (MOM, updated 12 January 2026).

A paid sick day is paid at the gross rate: 12 × monthly gross salary ÷ (52 × days worked a week), S$138.46 a day on S$3,000 for a 5-day week.

The hospitalisation figure includes the outpatient days: someone who has used 14 outpatient days in a year has 46 days of hospitalisation leave left, not 60. Outpatient sick leave needs a medical certificate from a doctor registered under the Medical Registration Act or a dentist registered under the Dental Registration Act; hospitalisation leave needs one from a doctor who can admit patients to hospital.

Worked example

Joined 2 March 2026, worked out on 20 July 2026: 4 completed months, so 8 outpatient days and 30 hospitalisation days. The steps start on 2 June (5 and 15), 2 July (8 and 30), 2 August (11 and 45) and 2 September 2026 (14 and 60).

With 3 outpatient days used, 8 − 3 = 5 outpatient days are left and 30 − 3 = 27 hospitalisation days. On a S$3,000 monthly gross salary and a 5-day week, the gross rate is 12 × 3,000 ÷ (52 × 5) = 36,000 ÷ 260 = S$138.46 a day, so the 3 paid sick days cost S$415.38.

What to check every month

  • Months of service, not the calendar. The entitlement steps up on the monthly anniversary of the start date until 6 months.
  • Outpatient days count twice. Take them off both the outpatient total and the hospitalisation total.
  • Payslip. Paid sick days are part of the month's salary, so nothing is deducted for them. Keep the count of days taken with each payslip; the payslip generator makes the itemised payslip.

Questions

How many days of sick leave do employees get in Singapore? After 6 months of service: 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave and 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave a year, and the 60 include the 14. Paid sick leave starts after 3 months of service.

How much sick leave do I get after 3 months? 5 days of outpatient sick leave and 15 days of hospitalisation leave. It rises to 8 and 30 at 4 months, 11 and 45 at 5 months, and 14 and 60 from 6 months.

Is outpatient sick leave part of hospitalisation leave? Yes. MOM says the 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave include the 14 days of outpatient sick leave, so outpatient days used also reduce the hospitalisation balance.

Who can issue the medical certificate? For outpatient sick leave, a doctor registered under the Medical Registration Act or a dentist registered under the Dental Registration Act. For hospitalisation leave, a doctor who can admit patients to hospital.

How is a paid sick day calculated? At the gross rate of pay: 12 x monthly gross salary divided by (52 x days worked a week). On S$3,000 for a 5-day week that is S$138.46 a day.

Do I get paid sick leave in my first 3 months? Not under the Employment Act. Paid sick leave starts after 3 months of service; the contract may give more.
