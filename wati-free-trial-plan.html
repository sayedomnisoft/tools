WATI free trial: a 7-day test plan (and what the trial cannot do)

Seven days go fast. The trial sends to your own number only and leaves a few things switched off, so decide what you are testing before the clock starts.

By Sayed Rashid, ex-auditor, 38 systems taken live. Prices and trial rules checked against WATI's own pages on 10 November 2026.

The short version
  • 7 days, and WATI's trial article says no payment details are needed.
  • Every message goes to your own WhatsApp number. Customers cannot be messaged.
  • You can build and test. You cannot submit templates for approval, save automation workflows or add your team.
  • Prepare first, then do one job a day. On day 7 you choose one of three decisions.

What the trial can and cannot do

You can

  • Use the team inbox and send messages and media files to your own number.
  • Send message templates to your own number.
  • Create, send and schedule campaigns to your own number, and see the overview dashboard.
  • Create and edit templates.
  • Test keyword actions and pre-built chatbots, and build a chatbot from scratch.
  • Try the Shopify, WooCommerce, Zoho and Google Sheets connections.
  • Use most API requests.

You cannot

  • Message anyone except your own number.
  • Submit a template for approval.
  • Save an automation workflow.
  • Add or manage users.
  • Use webhooks.
  • Use the HubSpot integration.
  • Change settings beyond the profile name and notifications.

Source: WATI's help-centre article on the free trial (support.wati.io, updated 7 September 2026), read on 10 November 2026.

So treat it as a one-seat test drive. It answers "would we use this, and is it easy enough?" It cannot answer "how do customers react?" Plan the week around the first question.

Day 7: three decisions

  1. Buy. The inbox was quick, the team took to it and the numbers from the fit check hold. Confirm the plan and billing, then clear the go-live checklist before the first payment.
  2. Wait. It worked, but something is not ready: the number, the Meta account or the person who will own it. Fix that first. WATI's trial article says the account remains after the 7 days and needs a paid plan to carry on.
  3. Stay on the free app. The team reached for the phone anyway, or the missed chats are too few to pay for a plan. That is a good result too: a week of testing instead of a year of paying.

What the trial cannot tell you

  • Whether Meta will approve your templates. Submission is switched off in the trial. Allow time for it after you subscribe.
  • How customers respond. Every message goes to your own number.
  • How the team shares the inbox day to day. User management is not part of the trial, which is why day 6 is a screen-share.
  • How long your number takes to connect. That depends on your Meta account and the number itself. The go-live checklist covers it.

If 7 days is not enough

WATI's trial article says extension requests are reviewed case by case by its team. Do not count on one. The preparation list above is the safer way to get a full week of testing out of the week.

Questions

Does the WATI free trial need a credit card?

WATI's help-centre article on the trial says no payment details are needed to start the 7-day trial.

Can I message my customers during the WATI trial?

No. Trial messaging is limited to your own WhatsApp number. You can send messages, templates and campaigns, but only to yourself.

Can I add my team during the WATI trial?

No. User management is not included in the trial. Treat it as a one-seat test and show the team your screen.

What happens when the WATI trial ends?

According to WATI's trial article, the account remains after the 7 days but needs a paid plan to keep working.

Can the WATI trial be extended?

WATI says extension requests are reviewed case by case by its team, so plan to finish your testing inside the 7 days.
