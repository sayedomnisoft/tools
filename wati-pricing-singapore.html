What is not in the plan price

  • Template message charges. The pricing page states additional charges for template messages, with the rate depending on the type: marketing, utility or authentication. Replies inside an open customer conversation are a different matter from messages you start. The WhatsApp API cost calculator works these out in detail.
  • Extra WhatsApp numbers. S$39 a month for each additional number.
  • The Shopify integration. Listed as an add-on at US$4.99 a month.
  • Extra users. Only on Pro and above. Growth cannot add any.

Yearly or monthly billing?

On Growth, yearly billing is S$119 a month against S$159 on monthly billing. That is S$480 less over 12 months. The pricing page also lists onboarding support with the yearly plan.

Monthly billing costs more but keeps the exit open. If you are not yet sure the team will use the inbox, one or two months on monthly billing is a fair price for finding out, and the 7-day trial before that is free.

Growth or Pro?

The question that decides it is simple: will a fourth person need a login this year? Growth includes 3 users and cannot add more, so the fourth person moves you to Pro, S$210 more a month on yearly billing. The other reasons to start on Pro are in the tick boxes above: very large broadcast volumes, HubSpot, webhooks, chat support at any hour, and routing chats between teams.

If none of those apply and three logins are enough, Growth is the plan. You can see every difference on WATI's own pricing page, which is the only place the current figures live.

Questions

How much is WATI per month in Singapore?

On 10 November 2026 WATI's pricing page showed Growth at S$119 a month billed yearly or S$159 billed monthly, for 3 users, and Pro at S$329 a month billed yearly or S$429 billed monthly, for 5 users. Template message charges are added on top.

Does WATI charge a setup fee?

WATI's pricing page lists zero setup fees and a 7-day free trial.

Does WATI charge per message?

Template messages carry additional charges on every plan, and the rate depends on whether the message is marketing, utility or authentication. Check WATI's rate card for the current rates in your country.

Can I add a fourth user on the WATI Growth plan?

No. Growth includes 3 users and has no option to add more. A fourth user needs the Pro plan, which includes 5 users and allows extra users at S$35 each a month.

Is yearly billing cheaper than monthly?

Yes. On Growth the difference is S$40 a month, or S$480 over 12 months. On Pro it is S$100 a month, or S$1,200 over 12 months.
