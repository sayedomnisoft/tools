Three ways to bring a number

1. A new number

The simplest route. A number that can take an SMS or a call for the code, and is not on an active WhatsApp account, connects without touching your current setup. The cost is telling customers about a new number.

2. Your current number, moved

Customers keep the number they know. The WhatsApp account on it has to be deleted first, and WATI's article says to wait at least 3 minutes before connecting. Deleting the account removes the chats in the app, so export anything you need before you do it, and pick a quiet hour.

3. Coexistence

This keeps the WhatsApp Business app working on the same number while it is connected to WATI. WATI lists the conditions: the number is active in the WhatsApp Business app, the app is version 2.24.17 or later, you have a Facebook Business Page with admin access, and you are in a supported country. Check the country list in WATI's article before you plan around it.

The connection itself

WATI calls the process Embedded Signup. In short: choose Embedded Signup and connect, pick the display name, continue with the Facebook account that manages your business, enter the phone number, choose a text message or a phone call for the code, type the code in and finish. With everything on the list above ready, the steps themselves are short. Without it, each missing item stops the process where it stands.

What usually blocks it

  • Nobody has admin access to Meta Business Manager. Find the admin this week, not on the day.
  • The website does not show who you are. Add the business name, contact details and address to a public page.
  • The number is still attached to a WhatsApp account. Decide between moving it and Coexistence first.
  • Someone tries it on a phone. Use a laptop with Chrome.
  • Business verification has not started. Messaging waits for it, so start as soon as you subscribe.

Why do this before paying?

Because WATI lists a paid subscription as a requirement for connecting the number, the paying comes first. Every day spent finding the Meta admin or fixing the website after that is a day of the plan you cannot use yet. Tick the list, then pay.

Questions

Do I need a new number for WATI?

No. You can use a new number, move your current number after deleting the WhatsApp account on it, or use Coexistence to keep the WhatsApp Business app on the same number if you meet WATI's conditions.

Can I keep the WhatsApp Business app and use WATI on the same number?

Yes, through Coexistence. WATI lists the conditions: the number is active in the WhatsApp Business app, the app is version 2.24.17 or later, you have a Facebook Business Page with admin access, and your country is supported.

Do I need a website to connect a WhatsApp Business API number?

WATI asks for an active, public website showing your business name, products or services, contact details and address. You can begin with a Facebook Page or Instagram Business profile, but a website is needed for Facebook Business verification.

Do I have to pay WATI before connecting my number?

Yes. WATI lists an active paid subscription as a requirement for connecting a business number. The free trial works without connecting it, by sending to your own WhatsApp number.

Can I set up the WhatsApp number from my phone?

WATI's setup article asks for a laptop or desktop with Google Chrome, signed in to both Facebook and WATI.
