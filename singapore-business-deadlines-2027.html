Short answer

For a Singapore private company with a 31 December year end, standard GST quarters and staff, the 2027 dates are: GST returns and payment by 31 January, 30 April, 31 July and 31 October; CPF contributions by the 14th of each month (15 February, 15 March, 16 August and 15 November when the 14th is a weekend); IR8A employment income by 1 March; ECI by 31 March; the AGM by 30 June; the annual return to ACRA by 31 July; Form C-S or Form C by 30 November.

Sources: IRAS, CPF Board and ACRA. Pick another year end below for your own dates.

CPF is due by the 14th in every other month too (14 January, 14 April, 14 May, 14 June, 14 July, 14 September, 14 October and 14 December). Several dates fall on a weekend: IRAS lists 31 January, 31 July and 31 October as they are, so file before the weekend.

The rules behind each date

  • GST: the return and payment are due one month after the end of each accounting period. With GIRO, IRAS deducts the payment on the 15th of the following month (the next working day if that is a weekend or holiday). A late return costs S$200 at once and S$200 for every further completed month, up to S$10,000 a return. Sources: IRAS due dates (updated 9 October 2026) and late filing (updated 10 August 2026).
  • CPF: contributions for a month are due on the last day of that month, and CPF Board takes enforcement action if they are not paid by the 14th of the following month, or the next working day when the 14th is a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday. Late payment interest is 1.5% a month. Source: CPF Board (updated 21 January 2025).
  • IR8A: employers in the Auto-Inclusion Scheme submit their employees' income to IRAS by 1 March; other employers give staff the IR8A by 1 March. AIS is compulsory with 5 or more employees. Source: IRAS AIS (updated 20 August 2026).
  • ECI: within 3 months of the financial year end, unless the company qualifies for the ECI filing waiver. Source: IRAS basic guide to corporate income tax (updated 20 October 2026).
  • Form C-S, C-S (Lite) or C: by 30 November every year, for the year of assessment that covers the previous financial year. Source: IRAS (updated 10 August 2026).
  • AGM: non-listed companies within 6 months of the year end. A private company does not need one if it sends its financial statements to all members within 5 months of the year end, or if it has chosen to dispense with AGMs. Source: ACRA (updated 11 February 2026).
  • Annual return: non-listed companies within 7 months of the year end; 8 months for those with a share capital and an overseas branch register. Late filing costs S$300 within three months and S$600 after that. Sources: ACRA deadlines (updated 14 May 2026) and penalties (updated 14 October 2026).

Questions

When is the GST return due in Singapore? One month after the end of each GST accounting period. For standard quarters: 30 April (January to March), 31 July (April to June), 31 October (July to September) and 31 January (October to December). The payment is due on the same date; with GIRO it is deducted on the 15th of the following month.

When must CPF contributions be paid? They are due on the last day of the month, and must be paid by the 14th of the following month to avoid enforcement action. When the 14th is a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, the deadline moves to the next working day: in 2027 that is 15 February, 15 March, 16 August and 15 November.

What is the IR8A deadline for 2027? 1 March 2027, for the income employees earned in 2026. Employers in the Auto-Inclusion Scheme submit it to IRAS; others give the IR8A to their employees by that date.

When is Form C-S due? By 30 November every year. In 2027 that is Form C-S, Form C-S (Lite) or Form C for YA 2027, covering the financial year that ended in 2026.

When is the ECI due? Within 3 months of the financial year end, unless the company qualifies for the ECI filing waiver. A 31 December 2026 year end means 31 March 2027.

When is the ACRA annual return due? Within 7 months of the financial year end for a non-listed company, or 8 months if it has a share capital and an overseas branch register. A 31 December 2026 year end means 31 July 2027. Late filing costs S$300, or S$600 if more than three months late.

Does every private company have to hold an AGM? No. A private company does not need an AGM if it sends its financial statements to all members within 5 months of the year end, or if it has chosen to dispense with AGMs. Otherwise a non-listed company holds it within 6 months of the year end.
