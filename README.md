# Free tools for Singapore business owners

Ten free calculators and checkers, built by [Sayed Rashid](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sayedomni)
for Singapore SMEs in trading, distribution and manufacturing.

**Live at [tools.gantry.work](https://tools.gantry.work/)** — no signup, no email capture, nothing stored.

## GST InvoiceNow (Singapore)

IRAS is phasing in the GST InvoiceNow Requirement between 2026 and 2031. These four answer the
questions that come up in order:

| Tool | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| [InvoiceNow deadline checker](https://tools.gantry.work/invoicenow-deadline.html) | When does the requirement actually hit my business? |
| [Software readiness checker](https://tools.gantry.work/invoicenow-software.html) | Is my accounting software one of the 203 accredited solutions? |
| [Free InvoiceNow packages](https://tools.gantry.work/invoicenow-free.html) | Can I comply without paying anything? |
| [IRAS grants](https://tools.gantry.work/invoicenow-grants.html) | What can I actually claim — S$1,000, S$5,000, or the Queen Bee route? |
| [Providers vs Access Points](https://tools.gantry.work/invoicenow-providers.html) | 203 solution providers, 47 Access Points — who do I call? |

## How they work

Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No build step, no framework, no analytics, no cookies.
Every calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Figures come from published
IRAS and IMDA sources and are dated on each page.
