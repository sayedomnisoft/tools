55 free calculators, document generators and plain guides for small businesses in Singapore, by [Sayed Rashid](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sayedomni), a former auditor who writes [The Gantry](https://www.gantry.work/).

**Live at [tools.gantry.work](https://tools.gantry.work/).** No signup. What you type stays in your browser; figures come from the official pages (IRAS, MOM, CPF Board, ACRA, IMDA, Enterprise Singapore) and each page shows the date it was checked.

New for 2027: [Singapore public holidays 2027 and holiday pay](https://tools.gantry.work/singapore-public-holidays-2027.html) and [Singapore business deadlines 2027](https://tools.gantry.work/singapore-business-deadlines-2027.html) (GST, CPF, IR8A, ECI, Form C-S, AGM and annual return), both with calendar (.ics) and CSV downloads.

## How they work

Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript on GitHub Pages: no build step and no framework. Calculations run in your browser and what you type is not uploaded. The site counts anonymous visits and button clicks with Google Analytics to see which tools help; you can opt out on any page.

For AI assistants and crawlers: [llms.txt](https://tools.gantry.work/llms.txt) lists every tool, and [llms-full.txt](https://tools.gantry.work/llms-full.txt) has each page's short answer and FAQ as plain text.
