Singapore Public Holidays 2027: Calendar and Holiday Pay

The 11 public holidays for 2027 as MOM published them, what each one means for staff on a 5, 5.5 or 6-day week, the holiday pay for anyone who works one, and the list as a calendar file, a spreadsheet or a printout. Free, no signup, nothing leaves your browser.

Short answer

Singapore has 11 public holidays in 2027 (MOM, page updated 19 June 2026). Chinese New Year falls on Saturday 6 and Sunday 7 February, so Monday 8 February is a public holiday for employees whose rest day is Sunday. Three holidays fall on a Saturday (6 February, 1 May and 25 December): staff on a 5-day week get another day off or one extra day's pay for each.

Anyone who works on a public holiday is owed an extra day's pay at the basic rate: 12 × monthly basic ÷ (52 × working days a week). On a S$3,000 basic salary over 5 days that is S$138.46.

Long weekends in 2027, and the week to plan cover for

  • Five three-day weekends for staff on a Monday to Friday week: New Year (Fri 1 to Sun 3 January), Chinese New Year (Sat 6 to Mon 8 February), Good Friday (Fri 26 to Sun 28 March), Hari Raya Haji (Sat 15 to Mon 17 May) and National Day (Sat 7 to Mon 9 August).
  • Two Thursday holidays, Vesak Day (20 May) and Deepavali (28 October). One day of leave on the Friday gives a four-day weekend, so expect leave requests for 21 May and 29 October.
  • The week of 17 May. Hari Raya Haji (Monday) and Vesak Day (Thursday) fall in the same week. Three days of leave (18, 19 and 21 May) give nine days off in a row, from Saturday 15 to Sunday 23 May. If your business runs on a small team, decide early who covers that week.

What employers owe on a public holiday

Employees covered by the Employment Act get 11 paid public holidays a year. MOM's rules (public holidays: entitlement and pay, updated 30 July 2025):

The holiday falls onIf the employee does not workIf the employee works
A working dayPaid day off at the gross rate (already in the monthly salary)An extra day's salary at the basic rate, plus overtime for hours beyond normal hours. By agreement, another working day off instead.
A non-working day (Saturday on a 5-day week)Another day off, or one extra day's salary at the gross rateOvertime for the hours worked, plus one extra day's gross salary or another day off for the holiday
A rest day (usually Sunday)The next working day is the paid holidayRest-day pay and any overtime; the next working day is still the paid holiday

An employee who is absent without consent or a reasonable excuse on the working day just before or after the holiday is not entitled to the holiday pay. Employees not covered by Part 4 of the Employment Act can be given time off in lieu instead of the extra day's pay, by agreement. Staff outside the Employment Act follow their contract.

How the day's pay is worked out

For a monthly-rated employee, MOM uses the same formula with two different salary figures (MOM, monthly and daily salary, updated 8 December 2025):

  • Basic rate for 1 day = 12 × monthly basic rate of pay ÷ (52 × average days worked a week). Used for working on a public holiday.
  • Gross rate for 1 day = 12 × monthly gross rate of pay ÷ (52 × average days worked a week). Used for a holiday that falls on a non-working day and is paid out instead of given as a day off. Gross pay includes allowances in the contract, but not overtime, bonuses, the AWS, productivity incentives, or travel, food and housing allowances.

Worked example: a 5-day week, S$3,000 basic salary and S$300 of contractual allowances (S$3,300 gross). The basic rate for a day is 12 × 3,000 ÷ (52 × 5) = 36,000 ÷ 260 = S$138.46, so working on National Day adds S$138.46 to the month's pay. The gross rate for a day is 39,600 ÷ 260 = S$152.31. The three Saturday holidays in 2027 then cost either three extra days off or 3 × S$152.31 = S$456.92 in pay.

Questions

How many public holidays does Singapore have in 2027? 11: New Year's Day (1 January), Chinese New Year (6 and 7 February), Hari Raya Puasa (10 March), Good Friday (26 March), Labour Day (1 May), Hari Raya Haji (17 May), Vesak Day (20 May), National Day (9 August), Deepavali (28 October) and Christmas Day (25 December).

When is Chinese New Year 2027 in Singapore? Saturday 6 and Sunday 7 February 2027. Because the second day falls on a Sunday, Monday 8 February 2027 is a public holiday for employees whose rest day falls on 7 February.

What happens when a public holiday falls on a Saturday? For staff on a 5-day week, Saturday is a non-working day, so they get another day off or one extra day's salary at the gross rate. In 2027 that applies to 6 February, 1 May and 25 December. For staff who work Saturdays, it is simply a paid day off.

What happens when a public holiday falls on a Sunday? If Sunday is the rest day, the next working day becomes the paid public holiday. In 2027 that is Monday 8 February, after Chinese New Year on Sunday 7 February.

How much must I pay an employee who works on a public holiday? An extra day's salary at the basic rate of pay, on top of the normal monthly salary, plus overtime for hours beyond normal hours. By mutual agreement you can give another working day off instead. For a monthly-rated employee the day's basic rate is 12 x monthly basic / (52 x days worked a week).

Is public holiday pay based on basic or gross salary? Working on a public holiday is paid at the basic rate. A holiday on a non-working day that is paid out instead of given as a day off is paid at the gross rate, which includes contractual allowances.

What are the long weekends in Singapore in 2027? For a Monday to Friday week: 1 to 3 January, 6 to 8 February, 26 to 28 March, 15 to 17 May and 7 to 9 August. Vesak Day (20 May) and Deepavali (28 October) fall on Thursdays, so one day of leave gives a four-day weekend.
