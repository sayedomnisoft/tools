How the check decides

There are two tests, and passing either one counts.

The money test. Missed chats a week, times the share that would have bought, times your order value, turned into a month. If that is more than the monthly price of a shared inbox for your team size, the inbox pays for itself. The price used is WATI's yearly-billed plan for your number of people: S$119 for up to 3, S$329 for 4 or 5, and S$35 for each person after that.

The mess test. Points for the number of people replying, how the chats are shared, how busy the day is, whether you broadcast, and whether you can trace who said what. A high score means the way you work has outgrown one phone, even if you cannot put a dollar figure on it yet.

If you pass neither, the page says so and gives you the free fixes instead. A tool you do not need is a monthly bill, not a solution.

What a shared inbox changes

  • One number, many logins. Customers keep one number. Each person on your team answers from their own screen.
  • Every chat has an owner. Chats are assigned, tagged and followed up, so "I thought you replied" stops.
  • The history stays with the business. When someone leaves, their chats do not leave with their phone.
  • Updates go out in one go. Order updates and announcements are sent as broadcasts, not pasted one by one.

What it does not fix

It will not make a slow team fast. If nobody is responsible for the inbox in each half of the day, chats will sit unanswered in a paid tool just as they did on a phone. Name the owner first. That costs nothing and it is the first thing we check in any setup.

Questions

Is the WhatsApp Business app enough for a small business?

For one person replying, usually yes. Quick replies, labels and an away message cover most needs. It starts to strain when two or more people share the same customer chats and nobody can see who answered what.

When does a shared WhatsApp inbox pay for itself?

When the orders you lose to late or missed replies are worth more each month than the plan. Multiply missed chats a week by the share that would have bought and by your order value, then compare that monthly figure with the plan price for your team size.

Can two people use one WhatsApp number?

Yes, in two ways. The free WhatsApp Business app lets you link extra devices to one number, which works for a couple of people but shows no owner for each chat. A shared inbox on the WhatsApp Business API gives each person a login, with chats assigned and tracked.
