This tool follows the published IRAS schedule, but your official date comes from IRAS — they notified businesses registered before 2026 from mid-2026, and they publish a GST InvoiceNow Implementation Date Calculator. If your business had no GST returns for periods ending in 2025, IRAS will tell you your date later. Always confirm before you plan around it.
