IF IT GOES BADLY

This is arithmetic, not a promise. It assumes the hours you win back turn into real work or real capacity — if the time just gets absorbed elsewhere, the money never shows up on your P&L. The 10 and 15 year figures include one major refresh in year 10 costing 40% of the original setup, because no system runs untouched for fifteen years. The sensitivity figures assume the same salary growth throughout. Treat the result as a sanity check, not a business case, and check the accounting treatment with your own accountant.
