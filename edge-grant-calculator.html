How the EDGE grant works for an ERP

The EDGE Grant replaced PSG, EDG and MRA on 30 September 2026. For most activities, including ERP and other integrated enterprise systems, SMEs get up to 50% of qualifying costs and non-SMEs up to 30%. The 70% rate applies only to internationalisation and sustainability activities.

  • Caps: up to S$100,000 of grant per company per year (1 April to 31 March), with a sub-cap of S$30,000 for digital solutions, integrated enterprise systems and equipment.
  • Who qualifies: a business registered in Singapore with at least 30% local (Singaporean or PR) shareholding. An SME has group sales of up to S$100 million or up to 200 workers.
  • ERP rule: an integrated enterprise system must cover at least three core functions.
  • Timing: about 1 week to process with a pre-approved vendor, about 10 weeks otherwise. Claims for digital solutions and enterprise systems open from 1 November 2026.

Worked example: a 12-person SME buys an ERP with S$6,000 of year-one subscription and S$22,000 of implementation. 50% of S$28,000 is S$14,000, below the S$30,000 sub-cap, so the estimated grant is S$14,000 and the net cost S$14,000. A S$80,000 project would hit the sub-cap: 50% is S$40,000, but the grant stops at S$30,000.

Mistakes that cost the grant

  1. Signing or paying before you apply. Apply first, then sign.
  2. One lump-sum quotation. Ask the vendor to list subscription, configuration, data migration and training separately so the qualifying parts are clear.
  3. Fewer than three core functions. An accounting-only system is not an integrated enterprise system.
  4. Forgetting the cap is shared. Other digital projects this year draw from the same S$30,000.

Source: Enterprise Singapore EDGE Grant FAQ, checked 6 October 2026. This calculator gives an estimate; Enterprise Singapore decides the activity, support level and qualifying costs.

Questions

How much is the EDGE grant for ERP? For SMEs, up to 50% of qualifying costs for an integrated enterprise system such as an ERP, and up to 30% for non-SMEs, within a S$30,000 sub-cap for digital solutions and enterprise systems.

Is the EDGE grant 70%? Only for internationalisation and sustainability activities. ERP and digital projects are supported at up to 50% for SMEs.

Does EDGE replace PSG? Yes. PSG, EDG and MRA stopped taking new applications after 30 September 2026; projects already applied for continue under their original terms.

When can I claim the EDGE grant? Claims for digital solutions, integrated enterprise systems and equipment open from 1 November 2026. You pay the vendor first and claim afterwards.
