Questions

Are these business tools really free? Yes. Your first three uses need nothing from you. After that, the tools stay open for anyone who asks for the free 2-minute video, books the free 20-minute review, or subscribes to The Gantry (the welcome email carries the link that reopens them). All three are free.

Is my data safe? Everything runs in your browser. Your files and entries are never uploaded or stored on a server; your business details are remembered only on your own computer.

Do the tools work outside Singapore? Yes. Currency and tax rate are editable. Singapore specifics such as 9% GST, tax invoices, PayNow and InvoiceNow are built in as defaults.
